Pack Journey

Illustration and visual development for a three-step in-game DTC experience, combining character-driven storytelling, event branding, and promotional art.

Category
Monetization Illustration
Format
3 Steps DTC
Includes

The core

3 Steps DTC

Three screens track the player's progress through the rounds toward the grand prize. Each step is shown as the final screen and its animated version.

01

Step 1: Setting off

The wizard sets out by boat. Both rounds are still ahead, with the grand prize waiting at the end of the route.

02

Step 2: Into the forest

The first round is complete. The wizard lights the way through the forest, staff raised.

03

Step 3: The grand prize

Both rounds are complete, and the wizard reveals the grand prize: a bag full of packs.

Process

Concept Exploration

Each scene was developed in black and white inside the event frame, setting composition, staging and the character's pose for every step.
